# Study plan

## Seven-day plan

| Day | Read | Lab |
|-----|------|-----|
| 1 | Foundations | Labs 0–1 |
| 2 | Branching and review | Lab 2 |
| 3 | Forges and protection | Lab 3 (policy design on paper) |
| 4 | History and incidents | Lab 4 |
| 5 | Releases and GitOps | Lab 5 |
| 6 | Q&A out loud | Optional Lab 6 (GitHub) |
| 7 | Capstone | Capstone |

## Rubric

| Level | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| Tutorial | You commit and push |
| Working | You branch, PR, and address review |
| Proficient | You use reflog, revert, bisect; you never force-push `main` |
| Advanced | You design protection rules, CODEOWNERS, release tags, and secret response |
| Teaching | A junior can follow your branching policy without asking Slack |
